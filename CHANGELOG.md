# Langur change history

These notes summarize dated local project records. Dates refer to development work, not confirmed public App Store releases.

## 1.0.5 (10) — August 20, 2026

- 115,395 dictionary entries; source examples for 21,121 entries.
- Lazy form index for broader inflected-word lookup.
- Source-backed meanings and examples; removed unverified conjugation generation.
- Removed runtime enrichment and the old account flow.
- Added Tatoeba attribution and corrected visible Turkish/German characters.
- Recorded runtime bundle reduction from 191 MB to 86 MB.

## July 14, 2026

- Visible, localized warnings for missing or unreadable dictionary resources.
- Reverse-index language and entry-count validation.

## May 25, 2026

- Phrase and compound lookup corrections.
- Related searches restricted to dictionary-backed results.
- Improved translation and example consistency.
